About the wire

A market wire that listens for one thing—a name. When the President says a company out loud, the tape tends to answer.

TrumpSays logs every time the U.S. President names a public company in a speech, a briefing, or on Truth Social, then pairs the headline with the stock’s move that day. No commentary, no spin — every story links out to the original source.
