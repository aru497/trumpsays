About the wire

A market wire that listens for one thing—a name. When the President says a company out loud, the tape tends to answer.

TrumpSays logs every time the U.S. President names a public company — on Truth Social, in a press conference, a speech, or the day’s news — then charts the stock’s move around the mention. No commentary, no spin; every story links out to the original source.
